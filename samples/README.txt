Sample corpus of Katalis Responde Community
===========================================

These documents describe a fictional small business, Café La Horquilla, a café and bicycle
workshop. They exist so the tests, the manual verification and the RAM measurement of this
repository have something real to ingest.

They were written for this repository. They are not the book of Construye, not golden cases, not
holdout data, not a customer document and not prices of a real business.

- cafe-la-horquilla.md: hours, prices and policies, in Spanish.
- bike-workshop-policies.md: bookings, storage, groups and guarantee, in English.
- notas-del-negocio.txt: payment, invoicing, pets, accessibility, in Spanish.
