Answer
**The guarantee:** Every repair carries a **90-day guarantee on the work**. Parts are covered by **the guarantee of their maker** (i.e., the manufacturer's warranty, not the workshop's own) [1].

**The ticket:** You're asked to bring it, but it isn't strictly required — without it, the workshop can still look up your repair **by the frame number** [1].
