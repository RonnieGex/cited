# Returns and warranty

At the workshop we take returns within thirty days of the purchase, as long as the part keeps its original
packaging and the ticket of the day. A change of mind needs no explanation: we look at the product, we test it
on the workbench and we refund it in the same way it was paid. The parts the workshop installed are not returned,
because the work is already done and the hours of the mechanic do not come back. If the part arrived with a knock
from the carrier, keep it as it is and write to us the same day: the review of the packaging is on us and the
replacement leaves the storeroom at no cost. The workshop checks the bicycle before returning it.

---

The workshop checks the bicycle before returning it. The laser calibration of the vacuum valve stays covered by
the extended warranty for two years, and the frame seal is checked at every service. The record of the service
travels by mail with the date and the name of the mechanic who signed the work, so you can ask for a copy when
you need it. Bring your ticket and the serial number of the frame to open the file.
