## 1. An answered question with its citations

```
> curl.exe -s -X POST http://127.0.0.1:3210/api/ask -H "content-type: application/json"
    -H "x-forwarded-for: 203.0.113.9" --data-binary "@q-price.json" -D h-answered.txt

HTTP/1.1 200 OK
content-type: application/json; charset=utf-8

{"status":"answered","answer":"Respuesta del proveedor de prueba: - Afinación de bicicleta: 380 pesos. [1]","citations":[{"n":1,"document":"cafe-la-horquilla.md","heading":"Precios","position":2,"excerpt":"Precios - Espresso: 35 pesos. - Café de olla: 45 pesos. - Pan dulce del día: 30 pesos. - Afinación de bicicleta: 380 pesos. - Cambio de cámara: 120 pesos."}]}
```

The question is `¿Cuánto cuesta una afinación de bicicleta?`. The answer carries `[1]` and the citation points at
`cafe-la-horquilla.md`, heading `Precios`, position 2, the passage that states the price; the only marker of the
answer has its citation and the citation is used.

```

{"status":"refused","answer":"No encuentro eso en los documentos de este negocio.","citations":[]}
```

The question is `¿Cuál es la capital de Australia?`. The status is `refused`, the citations are empty and the text is
the localized refusal, not the text of the model.

```
retry-after: 1087
{"status":"rate_limited","error":"more than RATE_LIMIT_PER_IP_PER_HOUR (30) questions from this address in an hour"}
```

its salted hash, as step 5.2 shows.
