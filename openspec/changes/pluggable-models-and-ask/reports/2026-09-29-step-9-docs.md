## 9.1 `docs/answering.md` and `.env.example`

### The document

`docs/answering.md` describes, in eleven sections: the route and the shape of the answer; the flow from the guards to
the citations; the prompt with its five rules and its passage delimiters; the parser of the citations; the refusal and
its two messages; the five guards and their defaults; the address of the visitor, the salted hash and `TRUST_PROXY`;
the nine providers with their variables, their default model and the license of their package; the license of every
package of the new dependency; the deterministic provider; the command line; and the tests that cover each piece.

Two facts of the tree that came before this change are stated exactly in section 8, because the documentation claims
no copyleft dependency: `jszip` (through `mammoth`) is `(MIT OR GPL-3.0-or-later)`, a dual license whose MIT option
this project uses, and the optional prebuilt binaries of `next` for sharp are `Apache-2.0 AND LGPL-3.0-or-later`.
LGPL-3.0 is neither GPL-3.0 nor AGPL-3.0, and nothing of this repository links against those binaries.

### The template

`.env.example` gains `CHAT_PROVIDER`, `TRUST_PROXY` and the defaults in comments: the nine provider names and the
key each one needs, the fact that `CHAT_MODEL` is optional, the five limits with their defaults (1000, 30, 500, 600)
and the retention of 30 days, and what `TRUST_PROXY=1` does and does not trust. Every variable of the file keeps an
empty value.

### The honesty rule had to be amended, and why

The README contract refuses any sentence of `docs/` that claims "answer" without a `Next` tag. That rule was written
when the answer was planned; the spec delta of this change amends it explicitly ("amended by Fable in
`pluggable-models-and-ask`: the answer with citations is available from this change on"). Documenting an available
capability without amending the rule would have been a lie in the other direction, so the guard and its test were
amended in the same commit as the document:

- `scripts/readme-graphics/honesty.mjs`: `answerWords` (which refused "answer" and "page") becomes `pageWords` (a
  passage keeps its document, its heading and its position, never a page) plus `plannedWords` (the voice agent, the
  admin panel, the widget, the one-click deploy), so the guard still refuses a planned capability presented as
  today's. `planificado` and `planificada` join the tags the guard accepts, because that is the word the Spanish twin
  uses; `honesty.d.mts` declares the two new constants.
- `tests/readme.test.ts`: the test `speaks of answers only in a line that carries Next` becomes
  `claims no page and no planned capability without its tag` and imports the two rules of the guard instead of
  duplicating its own; `offenders` accepts a null rule so that `docs/` is judged by the page rule and by the tags it
  already carries.
- Three lines of the two READMEs and one heading of `docs/security.md` said "reserved" of a planned capability; they
  now name the change that delivers it (`admin-and-public-ui`) or say `planned`, which is what the amended scenario
  asks for. The three bullets of `docs/security.md` that this change turns into facts (the limit of questions per
  address, the daily cap of model calls, the cap of tokens per answer) stopped saying **Planned** and now say
  **Done**, because a public threat model that says "planned" of a working guard is wrong in the same way.
