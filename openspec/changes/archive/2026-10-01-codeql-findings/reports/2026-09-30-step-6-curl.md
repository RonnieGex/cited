## 6.1 the login, the refusal and the upload

### The right password answers with its session cookie

```
$ curl.exe -s -i -X POST -H "content-type: application/json" -H "origin: http://127.0.0.1:3300" \
    -H "x-forwarded-for: 198.51.100.71" -d "{\"password\":\"cited-curl-codeql-2026\"}" \
    http://127.0.0.1:3300/api/admin/login
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
cache-control: no-store
content-type: application/json; charset=utf-8
set-cookie: cited_admin=1790858543702.9f8a34491ae63fb1c9c82a81daf702463bb61e864262550277e31e9682fd2fb6; Path=/; HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict; Max-Age=43200

{"status":"ok"}
```

The cookie carries `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Strict` and the twelve hours of the design, and no `Secure` because the
request arrives over `http` on `127.0.0.1`.

### The upload of a DOCX that holds `5 &lt; 6`, and the passages of the store

Two DOCX were built in the clone with the fixture of the suite, one whose paragraph reads `5 &lt; 6` as its author
typed it (`5 &amp;lt; 6` in the XML of the document) and one whose paragraph reads `5 < 6` (`5 &lt; 6` in the XML).
Both went through the upload of the panel with the session cookie:

```
$ curl.exe -s -X POST -H "cookie: cited_admin=<token>" -H "origin: http://127.0.0.1:3300" \
    -F "document=@.data/entidad.docx" http://127.0.0.1:3300/api/admin/documents
{"status":"ok","results":[{"name":"entidad.docx","state":"ready","passages":1,"failure":null}],
 "documents":[{"name":"entidad.docx","type":"docx","pages":null,"ingestedAt":"2026-10-01T00:42:41.095Z","passages":1}]}

$ curl.exe -s -X POST -H "cookie: cited_admin=<token>" -H "origin: http://127.0.0.1:3300" \
    -F "document=@.data/signo.docx" http://127.0.0.1:3300/api/admin/documents
{"status":"ok","results":[{"name":"signo.docx","state":"ready","passages":1,"failure":null}],
 "documents":[{"name":"entidad.docx","type":"docx","pages":null,"ingestedAt":"2026-10-01T00:42:41.095Z","passages":1},
              {"name":"signo.docx","type":"docx","pages":null,"ingestedAt":"2026-10-01T00:42:51.949Z","passages":1}]}
```

The passages of the store, read with `node:sqlite` in read-only mode:

```
$ node --experimental-strip-types .data/read-passages.mts
type: docx
text: "5 &lt; 6"
passages in the store:
entidad.docx #0: "5 &lt; 6"
signo.docx #0: "5 < 6"
```

The two doors of the scenario answer what the spec asks: the document whose author typed `5 &lt; 6` keeps it, and the
document whose author typed `5 < 6` reads it as a less-than sign and not as the start of a tag.
