   of the first control fell outside the viewport. Test defect: `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()` before the box. Commit `6926130`.
3. `brand.spec.ts:700`: `the switch over the band of the business: the texts the test measured` expected more than 3, received
   3 (`English`, `|`, `Espanol`). Test defect: the generic floor of four texts was wrong for a two-button switch. The floor
