## The two addresses

```

000        (exit 35: curl: (35) schannel: failed to receive handshake, SSL/TLS connection failed)

curl.exe -sS -o NUL -w "%{http_code}" --max-time 25 https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips
200        (exit 0)
```

The second address answers 200. The first one **could not be verified from this machine**: the name resolves
(`www.gnu.org` -> `209.51.188.116`), the TCP port 443 accepts a connection, and every TLS attempt dies before the
handshake with `curl: (35) schannel: failed to receive handshake, SSL/TLS connection failed` — the same with `-4`,
`--tlsv1.2`, `--tlsv1.3`, `--tls-max 1.2`, `-k`, `--http1.1`, `--ssl-no-revoke` and the `curl.exe` of Git for Windows;
`http://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html` answers `curl: (52) Empty reply from server`, and a `fetch` of Node 24 over
the same URL fails with a connect timeout after the SYN. It is a block of the machine's network toward `gnu.org` and not
a property of the address: the URL is the one the delta mandates, it is in the section, and it is written down here as
an UNKNOWN of this round (see `## Issues` of step 10.4) with every attempt that failed.
