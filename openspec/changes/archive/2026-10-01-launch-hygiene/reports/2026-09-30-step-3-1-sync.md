## What the commit carries

- `scripts/sync-agents.mjs`: reads every file under `ai-specs/agents/` (recursively, sorted), and for each of
  `.claude/agents`, `.codex/agents` and `.cursor/agents` removes the target and writes the copies byte for byte. It
  refuses to run when the root carries no `ai-specs/agents/` folder, when the source has no file, or when a copy would
  fall outside the root. It takes the root as its first argument and defaults to the repository root, which is the
  decision of the implementer that lets the test of 2.1 prove the sync in a temporary tree.
- `package.json`: `"agents:sync": "node scripts/sync-agents.mjs"`.
- The three symbolic links of the index (mode `120000`) replaced by nine regular files (mode `100644`) whose blobs are
  the blobs of the source, which is what makes them byte-for-byte copies.
