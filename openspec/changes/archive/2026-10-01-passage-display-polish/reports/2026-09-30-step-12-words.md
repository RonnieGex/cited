## Why the wording is not the sentence of decision 21

Decision 21 asks the texts to say that "the overlap is carried only when the next block overflows the passage, so `lead`
is `0` for most passages", and task 12.4 names `docs/answering.md` and the comments of the code as mine to correct. That
sentence is a step closer to the code than the old one, but it is still not what the code does: the overlap is carried
**never**. The branch of `add()` that would carry it is only read when `current` is empty and `carry` is not, and `add()`
always ends with text in `current` for a non-empty block, so the two are never true at once. Measured with the chunker of
`b65bb97` instrumented (the same measurement the report of task 12.3 prints): the branch is entered **0 times** over the
23 files of `docs/` and `samples/`, over 676 synthetic shapes of two and three paragraphs and over a block of one
unbreakable token of 1,500 characters.

Writing the sentence of decision 21 would have put a new false statement in the documentation of the product, which is
the defect the review found in the first place, so the texts say the measured thing instead. The difference is reported
in the `## Issues` of `2026-09-30-step-12-amendment.md`: the parenthesis of decision 20 and the sentence of decision 21
describe a mechanism the code does not have, and fixing the mechanism is a change of its own because it moves the
passages of every document.

Two places keep the old wording on purpose:

- `design.md` (its Context, corrected by Fable in Amendment 3) and the delta `openspec/changes/passage-display-polish/
  specs/answering/spec.md` ("the overlap the chunker carries when a block overflows a passage"): the text of the
  contract is not mine to edit.
- `docs/search.md:68` ("about 800 characters with 120 of overlap"): pre-existing in `main`, and outside the list of
  texts decision 21 names. It is recorded in the `## Issues` of the round.
