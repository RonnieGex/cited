## 7.1 The scenarios of the delta, in the browser

`e2e/passage-display.spec.ts` is new and runs in the `public` project against the server of port 3100 (the sample
corpus, the deterministic providers) and on `/embed` for the widget. Its cases:

| Case | What it measures | Where |
|---|---|---|
| the heading of the cited passage is shown once and its list is a list of five items | `[data-passage="heading"]` reads `Precios` once, the panel never contains `Precios - Espresso`, `[data-passage="item"]` counts 5, the first and the last item read `Espresso: 35 pesos.` and `Cambio de cámara: 120 pesos.`, and each item carries its own `.hl` | the public page at 1440 px and at 375 px |
| the passage of the public page speaks Spanish too | the same four readings with the page in Spanish | the public page at 1440 px and at 375 px |
| the words a passage repeats are muted and outside the highlighter | `[data-passage="lead"]` reads `Afinación de bicicleta: `, `.hl` reads `380 pesos.`, the muted element holds no `.hl` and the highlighter does not contain `Afinación` | the public page |
| the highlighter is inline and starts on the first line of its item, at 1440 px and at 375 px | the parent of `.hl` is an `LI`, its `display` is `inline`, its `box-decoration-break` is `clone`, the top of its band is within 4 px of the top of its line, and the five items carry five bands of their own | the public page |
| the widget opens the same passage | the heading once and the five items | `/embed` at 375 px |

The two scenarios of the owner are read in the walks that already exist, so no port is added:

| Case | What it measures | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "from zero to an answer, timed" | the mark beside the cited passage reads `1` — the citation's number, never the position of the passage | `e2e/setup.spec.ts`, `Try it` of port 3217 |
| "a document page lists its headings in reading order" | the passage of `Precios` carries its five items, the page carries the nine of its two lists, and the page paints no `[data-brand="citation-mark"]` at all | `e2e/setup.spec.ts`, `/admin/information/cafe-la-horquilla.md` |
| "de cero a una respuesta, en español, cronometrado" | the first suggestion names a heading of `cafe-la-horquilla.md` and the suggestions contain no heading of the English document; the mark beside the cited passage reads `1` | `e2e/setup-es.spec.ts`, `Try it` of port 3211 |

The Spanish string of the reopen link ("Abrir la configuración guiada otra vez") is pinned by
`tests/guided-setup-copy.test.ts`, which reads `lib/i18n/admin.ts`; the panel of the browser suite opens in English, so
the Spanish string of that link is not reachable from a browser walk of this suite.
