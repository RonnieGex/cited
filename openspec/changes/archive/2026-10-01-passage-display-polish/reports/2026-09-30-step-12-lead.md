## What changed

`e2e/fixtures/returns-and-warranty.md` and `e2e/fixtures/devoluciones-y-garantia.md` (`git diff --stat`: 2 files, 4
deletions) lost the `---` line that closed their block. The line was the way the fixture dodged the Major of the review:
`setextUnderline` reads `---` as a heading with no title, so it closed the block without changing the heading, and the
fixture held its two passages even while the chunker did not cut at an empty line. With the chunker of decision 18 the
empty line does the same work, so the fixtures are plain Markdown again and the passage boundary is the overflow of the
second paragraph.

**Where that `lead` comes from, said exactly.** The words are the sentence the document repeats at the boundary: the
first paragraph closes with `The workshop checks the bicycle before returning it.` and the second one opens with the
same sentence. They are not the overlap of the chunker: **the overlap of the chunker never reaches a passage**, in this
fixture or in any other. The branch of `add()` that would write it is only read when `current` is empty and `carry` is
not, and `add()` always ends with text in `current` for a non-empty block, so the two are never true at once: with the
chunker of this commit instrumented, the branch is entered **0 times** over the 23 files of `docs/` and `samples/`, over
676 synthetic shapes of two and three paragraphs and over a block of one unbreakable token of 1,500 characters. The
parenthesis of decision 20, "the case in which the chunker carries its overlap", names a case the code does not have.
What the fixture does have is the case decision 15 asks for: one section long enough to be cut into two passages by the
overflow of a paragraph, and a second passage that opens with the words the first one closes with.
