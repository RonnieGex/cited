## Why the corpus

The two Majors of `katalis-dev/tasks/revision-passage-display-polish-d.md` are (1) `cut()` of `lib/ingest/chunk.ts`
looks for the last space and not for the `\n` this change puts at a list join, so a block of more than 800 characters
cut at that join is cut a few words earlier than on `86b250f` — two real documents of the repository and 1,629 of 18,001
fuzzed documents with lists, and 26 of the 30 files of the corpus here before the fix; and (2) the golden fixture read
the live files of `docs/`, so the merge of `codeql-findings` (which edited `docs/security.md`) left the suite red.
Decision 23 answers both: the check reads only frozen copies of its inputs, and the corpus carries the shapes where the
cut at a list join happens, including the two documents the review named.

## The command that wrote the fixture

`scripts/chunk-golden.mts` walks the frozen inputs (nothing else) and writes the passages that one build of the chunker
returns for them. The fixture was written once, from the chunker of `86b250f`, with these commands in `<worktree>` (Node
`v24.21.0`, the one of `npx -y -p node@24`):

```powershell
<worktree> > cmd /c "git show 86b250f:lib/ingest/chunk.ts > <scratch>\chunk-86b250f.ts"
<worktree> > npx -y -p node@24 node scripts/chunk-golden.mts <scratch>\chunk-86b250f.ts tests/fixtures/chunker-golden.json "86b250f:lib/ingest/chunk.ts"
30 files, 385 passages
```

The extraction is the redirection of `cmd` because PowerShell's `>` re-encodes the output of `git show` as UTF-16 and
Node refuses to read TypeScript from it. The blob of `<scratch>\chunk-86b250f.ts` is
`0a647e68106bf600c230e23b26e1c18e6247d4fa`, equal to `git rev-parse 86b250f:lib/ingest/chunk.ts`, so the fixture was
written from the base chunker byte for byte. The third argument is the label the fixture carries,
`86b250f:lib/ingest/chunk.ts`; it holds no path of this machine.

`tests/fixtures/chunker-golden.json` (219,507 bytes): `label` `86b250f:lib/ingest/chunk.ts`, 30 files and 385 passages,
each with its `heading` and its `text`. The fixture that held the passages of the live documents was **replaced**, not
kept beside it (decision 23 and task 13.3).

## Decision 24: the first passage whose text differs

The old check returned `Math.min(actual.length, file.passages.length)` as soon as the two counts differed, without
comparing a passage (the fourth review, Minor 3: with the chunker of `c2f5d2a` it named `docs/admin.md, passage 30`).
The new check compares position by position up to the smaller count and only reports the counts when every common
passage is equal. Verified with the chunker of `c2f5d2a` copied over `lib/ingest/chunk.ts` (blob
`33d9b5564e21858a3fbbff6fbb1fae897dd2ab3f`), where the counts differ (30 against 33 passages for `docs/admin.md`) and
the first real difference is passage 1:

```text
AssertionError: the first difference is docs/admin.md, passage 1: The panel | The panel: expected { file:
'docs/admin.md', …(3) } to be null

  "actual": "The panel | The panel",
  "expected": "The panel | The panel The panel is the place where the owner of a fork sees what is configured, …",
  "file": "docs/admin.md",
  "position": 1,
```

The scratch comparison over the frozen corpus with the same mutant prints `docs/admin.md passage 1 (30 vs 33
passages)`, so the position the check names is the first passage whose text really differs. `lib/ingest/chunk.ts` was
restored to `7774c199c3f04e61e891b236ccc2c12a107da2c8` after the run, and `git status` shows no change to it.
