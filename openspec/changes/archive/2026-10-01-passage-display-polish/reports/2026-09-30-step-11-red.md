## Why a fixture

The Major of `katalis-dev/tasks/revision-passage-display-polish-b.md` is that no passage of the sample corpus repeats
the end of the one before it, so every citation of the walk has `lead: 0` and a mutant that passes `lead={0}` to the
owner's two views walks the whole browser suite green. I measured the other half of that fact while building the
fixture: the store of a real run holds `lead: 0` for every passage of the corpus, and so does the output of
`npm run ask` in the README (`[1] cafe-la-horquilla.md [Precios] position 2 lead 0`), because the overlap of
`chunk.ts` never reaches the text of a passage: the `carry` of `emit()` is only read by the `if` that opens `add()`
when `current` is empty, and `add()` never returns with an empty `current`, so no passage after the first of a
section starts with the end of the one before it. The fixture of decision 15 gives the two views a real lead the way
the text of a document can: its one section is long enough to be cut into two passages and its second passage opens
with the sentence that closes the first.

## The fixture

Two documents, one per language of the run, uploaded by the browser suite itself:

| File | Heading | Passages | The words that repeat | The first own word |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `e2e/fixtures/returns-and-warranty.md` | `Returns and warranty` | 2 (674 and 417 characters) | `The workshop checks the bicycle before returning it.` | `The laser calibration of the vacuum valve` |
| `e2e/fixtures/devoluciones-y-garantia.md` | `Devoluciones y garantía` | 2 (697 and 431 characters) | `El taller revisa la bicicleta antes de devolverla.` | `La calibración láser de la válvula de vacío` |

The section carries a `---` line between the two bodies: the chunker reads it as a heading line with no title, so it
closes the block without changing the heading of the section, and the section is stored as two passages of the same
heading. The second body opens with the sentence the first one closes with, so `leadLength` of the stored passages
gives the second passage a lead of 52 characters (English) and 50 (Spanish) instead of 0. The question
(`Is the laser calibration of the vacuum valve covered?` and `¿La calibración láser de la válvula de vacío?`) is
answered by the local double from that second passage, because those words appear in no other passage of the store:
I measured the ranking of the keyword search over a scratch store with `samples/` and both fixtures, and the hit is
`returns-and-warranty.md [Returns and warranty] position 1` in English and `devoluciones-y-garantia.md
[Devoluciones y garantía] position 1` in Spanish, the passage with the lead in both cases.
