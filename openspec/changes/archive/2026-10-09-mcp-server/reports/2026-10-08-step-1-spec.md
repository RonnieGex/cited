`202` without a body for a notification, one `application/json` object for a request, `405` for `GET`, the security
warning of the `Origin`, the protocol version header and the optional session), the lifecycle (initialization and
version negotiation), the tools (the `tools` capability, `inputSchema`, `outputSchema`, `annotations`, structured
content and the two kinds of error) and authorization (optional for an implementation, `WWW-Authenticate` on a `401`).
