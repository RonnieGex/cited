The seven cases of the new project `keys`, in their order:

```
ok 1 the page of AI and keys lists the providers with one honest line and a link
ok 2 the affiliate switch is off, the links are plain and the hosted offer is shown
ok 3 a rejected key is said in words and nothing is saved
ok 4 a chat provider is connected through its double and only its last four characters show
```

Two things were corrected in the spec while it was being made green, and both are recorded here because they were
wrong tests and not wrong code:

1. The case of the rejected key asserted that the key the owner pasted was not in the HTML. It stays in the password
   field until the owner fixes it, which is what the field is for; what must never appear is the text of the provider,
   and that is what the case now asserts, together with `v1:` and the disabled save.
2. The case of the keyword mode asserted the connected card right after the click. The panel confirms the save inline
   and the card is what the page renders from the store on the next visit, so the case now waits for the confirmation,
   reloads and then asserts the card. The click was also the moment a real defect showed: keyword mode was saved and
   the page still offered the form, because the state of the panel keyed "connected" on the provider and keyword mode
   has none. `lib/admin/provider-panel.ts` now names it by its mode, and the card shows "Search by words" as its name.
