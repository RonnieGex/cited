The new document, in English as the standard asks, with the ten sections the task names: the one-click agent, the
prompt with the lesson of Construye, the server tool, the signed URL and the key, the allowlist, the cap of voice
minutes, the variables, the privacy note, the browser side and the tests. It quotes the two pages of the

`ALLOWED_ORIGINS`.

### The graphics of the README

The status table and the roadmap of the README have to say the same, so the record and the templates moved with them:

- `scripts/readme-graphics/data.mjs`: the voice row is `Available` and links `openspec/specs/voice-agent/spec.md`.
- `scripts/readme-graphics/manifest.mjs`: `reason-voice` and `voice-teaser` show `Available` with no `Next` label, and
  their alt texts and the copy of the card say what the product does now. The alt of `how-it-works` loses "with the
  voice marked `Next`".
- `scripts/readme-graphics/{reason-voice,voice-teaser,how-it-works}.html`: the `Next` badges are gone and the copy is
  the new one.
- `scripts/readme-graphics/honesty.mjs`: `plannedWords` stopped refusing the words of a voice agent, because a voice
  agent is a capability of today now.
- `scripts/readme-graphics/roadmap.html` and the render script: the board of the roadmap is a little denser
  (`.roadmap`) and the canvas is 720 px high, the ceiling of decision 11, because the available column grew from seven
  rows to eight when the voice moved into it. `docs/readme-assets.md` records both facts.
- `scripts/readme-graphics/roadmap.html`: the note of the board said "the six rows of Available run today with the
  deterministic providers and no key", which stopped being true twice (there are eight, and the voice agent of a real
  installation needs the key of its owner). It now says that every available row is built and carries its tests.
