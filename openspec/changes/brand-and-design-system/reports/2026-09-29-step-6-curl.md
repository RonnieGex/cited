The two font files answer 200 **from the app**, with the sizes their hashes were computed over (32292 and 14808 bytes),
and the license is served next to them. The last two rows are not asked for by the task; they are there because the
second subset is part of the face and the license is part of the promise.

## The font is served by the app and by nobody else

```
$ curl.exe -sS http://127.0.0.1:3200/kit > kit.html
external font hosts in the HTML of /kit: 0
components of the kit in the HTML: 6

$ curl.exe -sS http://127.0.0.1:3200/_next/static/chunks/2nb-u5qmsgwek.css
bytes of the stylesheet: 14828
@font-face: 2
urls of the app: 2
urls of a font host: 0
```

The two faces of the stylesheet the app serves:

```css
@font-face{font-family:Outfit;font-style:normal;font-weight:100 900;font-display:swap;src:url(/fonts/outfit/outfit-latin.woff2)format("woff2");unicode-range:U+??,U+131,...}
@font-face{font-family:Outfit;font-style:normal;font-weight:100 900;font-display:swap;src:url(/fonts/outfit/outfit-latin-ext.woff2)format("woff2");unicode-range:U+100-2BA,...}
```

and the family on the root element:

```css
.font-sans{font-family:Outfit,system-ui,sans-serif}
```

The six markers of the kit are in the HTML of `/kit`, one each:
`data-kit="section-title"`, `data-kit="button-primary"`, `data-kit="button-secondary"`, `data-kit="input"`,
`data-kit="chip"` and `data-kit="panel"`.

The focus of the kit is in the served stylesheet too, which is what makes the outline of the browser real and not a
promise of the source:

```css
.focus-visible\:outline-2:focus-visible{outline-style:var(--tw-outline-style);outline-width:2px}
.focus-visible\:outline-offset-2:focus-visible{outline-offset:2px}
.focus-visible\:outline-lime:focus-visible{outline-color:var(--lime)}
.focus-visible\:ring-1:focus-visible{...}
.focus-visible\:ring-ink:focus-visible{--tw-ring-color:var(--ink)}
```
