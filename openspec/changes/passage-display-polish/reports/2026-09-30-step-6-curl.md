`lead: 0` again: the passage is the whole document `notas-del-negocio.txt` and the first of it, so it repeats nothing of
a passage before it. The provider is deterministic and answers with the first sentence of the passage that shares a word
with the question; both questions of this task land on a first passage of its section, which is why the two citations
carry `0`.

## Where a `lead` that is not zero is proved

A citation with a `lead` other than 0 comes from a passage that continues the one before it in the same document, and
the sample corpus has exactly one such passage: `cafe-la-horquilla.md` position 1, whose text starts with the carry of
position 0. No question of this task surfaces it, and the requirement is covered by the tests instead, at the code of
this same commit:

| Test | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `tests/answer-lead.test.ts` > "carries the length of the repeated words for a passage that continues the one before it" | the citation of a passage that continues its previous one carries `lead` equal to the repeated words, and `excerpt.slice(lead)` starts with its own first word |
| `tests/answer-lead.test.ts` > "is zero for the first passage of a section and for a passage with no neighbour" | a first passage or a passage with no previous one carries 0 |
| `tests/passage-view.test.tsx` > "measures the overlap the chunker makes" | `leadLength` measures the overlap the chunker writes |
| `tests/setup-ui.test.tsx` > "shows the heading of the cited passage once and its list as a list of five items" | the view draws the lead muted, outside the highlighter |
