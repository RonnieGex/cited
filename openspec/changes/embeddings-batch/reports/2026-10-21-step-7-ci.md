## Lesson recorded

Two red runs were caused by the implementer, not by the change: the audit drift was found by the pipeline because the
local gate of step 4 ran before the first push with the registry of that moment, and the type error reached the
pipeline because the local type check was not repeated after the last edit of the test. Both are recorded here as
they happened; the gate commands of the mandatory steps are to be rerun after the last edit of every file.
