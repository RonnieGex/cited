cannot even load, which is the red of the suite on `4b9b038`:

```

    --execArgv=--require=<the preload of the session> tests/audit-high.test.ts

 Test Files  1 failed (1)

$ Move-Item scripts/audit-high.mjs.red scripts/audit-high.mjs
```

The second red is the contract itself, and it is what the fix has to close: the guard run against the payload of the
measured base reports the one advisory that the tree of `4b9b038` still carries and no entry covers.

```
$ node scripts/audit-high.mjs --audit-file tests/fixtures/audit/tree-of-f644f85.json \
    --prod-audit-file tests/fixtures/audit/production-clean.json \
    --prod-tree-file tests/fixtures/audit/production-tree.json \
    --exceptions-file tests/fixtures/audit/exceptions-of-braces.json --today 2026-10-09

AUDIT: FAIL (1 problems)
exit 1
```

`scripts/gate-audit.mjs` runs that same command as one of its statements, so the red stays reproducible after the
guard exists.
